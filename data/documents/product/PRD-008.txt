Product ID: PRD-008

Product Overview

Analytics Dashboard is the centralized business intelligence, operational analytics, reporting, and observability platform within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The platform provides real-time visibility into AI systems, infrastructure operations, platform usage, reliability metrics, security posture, cost efficiency, and business outcomes.

The dashboard aggregates information from AetherAI Gateway, Enterprise RAG Service, Evaluation Engine, Guardrails Gateway, Agent Workflow Studio, Knowledge Hub, Reliability Platform, and supporting infrastructure components. Through unified reporting and visualization capabilities, stakeholders gain actionable insights into system performance, adoption trends, operational risks, and optimization opportunities.

Analytics Dashboard serves executives, engineering teams, product organizations, security personnel, compliance teams, and operational leadership.

Customer Problems

Organizations operating AI platforms frequently encounter challenges related to visibility and decision-making.

Fragmented Reporting

Operational information is often distributed across multiple tools and systems.

Limited Business Visibility

Stakeholders may struggle to understand how AI systems contribute to business outcomes.

Reliability Monitoring Challenges

Tracking quality, groundedness, hallucinations, and performance across services can be difficult.

Cost Management Complexity

Organizations require insight into AI spending, infrastructure utilization, and optimization opportunities.

Operational Decision-Making Gaps

Teams need timely access to accurate information for planning and response activities.

Compliance Reporting Requirements

Regulatory and governance processes often require detailed operational reporting.

Analytics Dashboard addresses these challenges through centralized intelligence and reporting.
