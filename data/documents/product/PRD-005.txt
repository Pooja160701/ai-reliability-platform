Product ID: PRD-005

Product Overview

Model Registry is the centralized model lifecycle management platform within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The platform serves as the authoritative system of record for all machine learning models, large language models, embedding models, evaluation models, fine-tuned models, and associated artifacts deployed across enterprise environments.

The registry provides version management, governance controls, approval workflows, deployment tracking, ownership assignment, evaluation integration, rollback support, and operational visibility throughout the model lifecycle.

By centralizing model governance and operational management, the platform ensures consistency, auditability, compliance, and reliability across all AI systems.

Customer Problems

Organizations operating AI systems frequently encounter governance and lifecycle management challenges.

Model Sprawl

Models are often distributed across multiple repositories and environments, making management difficult.

Version Tracking Difficulties

Teams may struggle to identify which model version is deployed, approved, or under evaluation.

Limited Governance

Organizations require structured controls to manage approvals, ownership, compliance, and deployment decisions.

Operational Visibility Gaps

Understanding model usage, performance, and deployment history is often challenging.

Rollback Complexity

Restoring previous model versions can become difficult without centralized lifecycle tracking.

Evaluation Traceability Issues

Linking models to evaluation outcomes and deployment decisions is frequently inconsistent.

Model Registry addresses these challenges through centralized lifecycle governance.
