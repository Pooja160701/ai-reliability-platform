Product ID: PRD-004

Product Overview

Evaluation Engine is the centralized AI quality assurance and benchmarking platform within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The platform continuously evaluates language models, retrieval systems, agent workflows, prompts, embeddings, guardrails, and production AI applications to ensure quality, reliability, safety, and business alignment.

The Evaluation Engine serves as the authoritative decision system for release approval, regression detection, hallucination monitoring, groundedness validation, safety verification, and operational readiness assessments.

By providing automated evaluation workflows and standardized benchmarks, the platform enables organizations to deploy AI systems with confidence while maintaining governance, compliance, and reliability standards.

Customer Problems

Organizations frequently encounter challenges when validating AI systems at scale.

Lack of Quality Visibility

Teams often struggle to measure model quality consistently across environments and releases.

Hallucination Risks

AI systems may generate unsupported responses without detection mechanisms.

Regression Detection Difficulties

Changes to prompts, models, retrieval systems, or configurations can introduce hidden quality degradations.

Safety Validation Challenges

Organizations require repeatable testing for prompt injection resistance, policy compliance, and output safety.

Release Governance Gaps

Deployments frequently occur without objective quality measurements.

Benchmark Inconsistency

Different teams often use different evaluation approaches, reducing comparability and reliability.

Evaluation Engine addresses these challenges through centralized benchmarking and automated validation.
