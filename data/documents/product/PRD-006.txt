Product ID: PRD-006

Product Overview

Guardrails Gateway is the centralized AI safety, security, policy enforcement, and compliance platform within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The platform operates as a protective control layer between users, applications, retrieval systems, agents, and language models, ensuring that all AI interactions comply with organizational policies, security requirements, regulatory obligations, and reliability standards.

The gateway inspects, validates, filters, and governs requests and responses flowing through enterprise AI systems. It provides protection against prompt injection attacks, unsafe outputs, data leakage, policy violations, adversarial inputs, and operational misuse.

Guardrails Gateway serves as the primary enforcement mechanism supporting AetherAI Gateway, Enterprise RAG Service, Agent Workflow Studio, Knowledge Hub, Evaluation Engine, and Reliability Platform.

Customer Problems

Organizations deploying AI systems face several significant operational and security challenges.

Prompt Injection Attacks

Malicious users may attempt to manipulate model behavior through adversarial instructions.

Data Leakage Risks

Sensitive information may be exposed through model interactions without appropriate controls.

Policy Compliance Challenges

Organizations require consistent enforcement of business, security, legal, and operational policies.

Unsafe Content Generation

AI systems may generate harmful, misleading, inappropriate, or non-compliant outputs.

Governance Gaps

Organizations often lack centralized policy enforcement mechanisms.

Operational Risk

Uncontrolled AI behavior can create security, compliance, reputational, and reliability concerns.

Guardrails Gateway addresses these challenges through centralized AI governance and policy enforcement.
