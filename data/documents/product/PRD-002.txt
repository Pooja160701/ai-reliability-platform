Product ID: PRD-002

Product Overview

Enterprise RAG Service is the flagship Retrieval-Augmented Generation platform within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The service combines enterprise knowledge retrieval, vector search, contextual grounding, large language model orchestration, citation generation, and reliability controls to deliver accurate, explainable, and trustworthy AI responses.

The platform enables organizations to build AI applications that answer questions using approved enterprise knowledge sources rather than relying solely on model training data. By augmenting language model responses with retrieved context, the service improves factual accuracy, reduces hallucinations, increases transparency, and enhances user trust.

Enterprise RAG Service serves as a foundational capability supporting customer-facing assistants, internal knowledge systems, agent workflows, operational support tools, compliance platforms, and enterprise search solutions.

Customer Problems

Organizations deploying AI systems frequently encounter challenges that impact reliability and business adoption.

Hallucinated Responses

Language models may generate unsupported or incorrect information when responding without relevant context.

Knowledge Accessibility Issues

Enterprise information is often distributed across multiple repositories, making discovery and utilization difficult.

Limited Explainability

Many AI systems provide answers without supporting evidence, reducing user confidence and increasing operational risk.

Knowledge Freshness Challenges

Model training data does not automatically reflect changing organizational information.

Compliance Concerns

Organizations require visibility into information sources used to generate responses.

Operational Complexity

Building and maintaining retrieval systems requires significant engineering investment.

Enterprise RAG Service addresses these challenges through retrieval-based grounding and enterprise knowledge integration.
