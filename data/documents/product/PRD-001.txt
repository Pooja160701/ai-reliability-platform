Product ID: PRD-001

Product Overview

AetherAI Gateway is the centralized AI access and orchestration platform that serves as the primary entry point for all enterprise AI workloads within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The platform provides secure, scalable, observable, and governed access to large language models, retrieval systems, agent frameworks, embedding services, evaluation engines, and AI infrastructure components.

The gateway abstracts model providers, enforces organizational policies, manages routing decisions, monitors usage, controls costs, and standardizes AI interactions across internal and customer-facing applications.

The platform is designed to support enterprise-scale AI adoption while ensuring security, reliability, governance, compliance, and operational visibility.

Customer Problems

Organizations adopting AI technologies often encounter several challenges:

Model Fragmentation

Different teams integrate directly with different model providers, creating inconsistent implementations and operational complexity.

Security Risks

Direct model access can expose organizations to prompt injection attacks, data leakage, policy violations, and governance gaps.

Cost Management Challenges

Without centralized controls, AI usage costs become difficult to monitor and optimize.

Operational Visibility Limitations

Organizations often lack centralized monitoring, auditing, and observability capabilities.

Vendor Lock-In

Applications become tightly coupled to individual model providers, limiting flexibility and increasing migration complexity.

Reliability Concerns

Model failures, outages, latency issues, and quality regressions can significantly impact business operations.

AetherAI Gateway addresses these challenges through centralized governance and intelligent orchestration.
