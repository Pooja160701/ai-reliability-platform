Title: Incident Escalation Workflow

Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the incident escalation workflow for production systems operating within the AetherAI Cloud platform. The objective is to ensure that operational incidents are identified, assessed, escalated, communicated, and resolved in a consistent and timely manner. A structured escalation process minimizes service disruption, protects customer workloads, accelerates recovery efforts, and supports organizational accountability.

This procedure applies to incidents affecting the Enterprise RAG Platform, LLM Gateway, Guardrails Gateway, Evaluation Engine, Knowledge Hub, Reliability Platform, AI agent services, and supporting AWS infrastructure.

Scope

This SOP applies to:

• Production service outages
• Platform performance degradation
• Security incidents
• AI inference failures
• Retrieval quality degradation
• Authentication failures
• Infrastructure failures
• Data processing interruptions
• Database incidents
• Customer-impacting operational events

All engineering, platform operations, MLOps, reliability, security, and management personnel must comply with this procedure.

Prerequisites

Prior to incident occurrence, the following capabilities must exist:

1. Centralized monitoring systems must be operational.
2. Alerting systems must be configured.
3. On-call schedules must be maintained.
4. Incident communication channels must be available.
5. Escalation contacts must be current.
6. Runbooks must be documented.
7. Service ownership assignments must be defined.
8. Audit logging must be enabled.
9. Incident management tools must be operational.
10. Severity classification guidelines must be published.

Monitoring signals include:

Incident Severity Classification

Severity 1 (Critical)

Characteristics:

• Complete production outage
• Major customer impact
• Revenue-impacting disruption
• Security breach
• Regulatory risk

Target Response Time:

15 minutes

Severity 2 (High)

Characteristics:

• Significant service degradation
• Partial outage
• Major workflow disruption
• High-priority customer impact

Target Response Time:

30 minutes

Severity 3 (Medium)

Characteristics:

• Limited customer impact
• Non-critical functionality affected
• Performance degradation

Target Response Time:

2 hours

Severity 4 (Low)

Characteristics:

• Minor operational issues
• Cosmetic defects
• Non-production concerns

Target Response Time:

1 business day

Step-by-Step Procedure

Step 1: Incident Detection

Incidents may be identified through:

• Automated monitoring alerts
• Customer reports
• Security detections
• Operational reviews
• Service ownership teams

The detecting individual must immediately record the incident.

Step 2: Initial Assessment

The responding engineer evaluates:

• Affected systems
• Customer impact
• Service availability
• Business impact
• Security implications

A preliminary severity level is assigned.

Step 3: Incident Declaration

An official incident record is created containing:

• Incident identifier
• Detection time
• Affected services
• Initial severity
• Responsible personnel
• Incident description

The incident becomes active.

Step 4: Incident Commander Assignment

For Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents, an Incident Commander must be assigned immediately.

The Incident Commander assumes coordination responsibility throughout the incident lifecycle.

Step 5: Level 1 Escalation

The incident is escalated to:

• On-call engineer
• Service owner
• Platform operations personnel

Initial mitigation efforts begin immediately.

Step 6: Level 2 Escalation

If resolution is not achieved within target response objectives:

Escalate to:

• Engineering manager
• Senior platform engineer
• Reliability engineering lead

Additional resources are assigned.

Step 7: Level 3 Escalation

For major incidents:

Escalate to:

• Director of Engineering
• Head of Platform Operations
• Security leadership

Cross-functional response teams may be activated.

Step 8: Executive Escalation

Severity 1 incidents require executive notification.

Stakeholders may include:

• Chief Technology Officer
• Chief Information Security Officer
• Executive Operations Team

Executive escalation supports strategic decision-making and customer communication.

Step 9: Incident Communication

Status updates must be provided according to severity.

Severity 1:

Every 30 minutes

Severity 2:

Every 60 minutes

Severity 3:

Every 4 hours

Updates must include:

• Current status
• Mitigation actions
• Recovery progress
• Estimated timelines

Step 10: Resolution Validation

Following mitigation:

Validate:

• Service availability
• Customer workflows
• Authentication systems
• Database functionality
• AI platform performance
• Monitoring telemetry

Recovery must be confirmed before incident closure.

Step 11: Incident Closure

The Incident Commander documents:

• Resolution summary
• Recovery actions
• Timeline of events
• Customer impact
• Escalation history

Formal closure approval is required.

Failure Handling

If recovery efforts fail:

1. Escalate to the next response level.
2. Expand response teams.
3. Consider rollback procedures.
4. Evaluate disaster recovery activation.
5. Initiate executive response processes.

Extended incidents require formal management oversight.

Risks

Failure to properly escalate incidents may result in:

• Extended downtime
• Increased customer impact
• Security exposure
• Revenue loss
• Regulatory concerns
• Operational confusion
• Delayed recovery

Risk mitigation depends on monitoring, training, communication, and clearly defined escalation paths.

Compliance Considerations

Incident management activities must support:

• Auditability
• Change management controls
• Security governance
• Operational transparency
• Regulatory reporting requirements

All incident records, communications, escalations, and resolution activities must be retained according to enterprise retention policies.

Summary

The Incident Escalation Workflow establishes a structured process for responding to operational events within AetherAI Cloud. Through clear severity definitions, escalation paths, communication standards, and accountability assignments, the organization can rapidly mobilize resources, minimize service disruption, and restore platform stability. Consistent adherence to this workflow is essential for maintaining reliability, customer trust, and operational excellence.
