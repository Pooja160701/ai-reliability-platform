Architecture ID: ARCH-010

Title: Disaster Recovery Architecture

Business Requirements

The Disaster Recovery Architecture establishes the framework for restoring critical services, infrastructure, applications, data platforms, AI workloads, and operational capabilities following disruptive events affecting the AetherAI Cloud environment. The architecture is designed to minimize downtime, reduce data loss, maintain business continuity, and ensure the rapid restoration of enterprise AI services.

The primary objective is to provide a resilient recovery strategy capable of supporting operational continuity during infrastructure failures, cloud service disruptions, security incidents, regional outages, application failures, and data corruption events.

The architecture must satisfy the following business requirements:

• Business continuity assurance
• Rapid service recovery
• Minimal data loss
• Multi-region resilience
• Automated recovery capabilities
• Recovery testing support
• Regulatory compliance readiness
• Customer service continuity
• Infrastructure reproducibility
• Operational reliability

The architecture supports the Enterprise RAG Platform, LLM Gateway, Guardrails Gateway, Evaluation Engine, Knowledge Hub, Agent Workflow Studio, Reliability Platform, Data Lake, Vector Search Platform, and supporting AWS infrastructure.

Architecture Overview

The Disaster Recovery Architecture follows a layered resilience model built upon redundancy, backup protection, replication strategies, Infrastructure-as-Code, automated recovery workflows, and regional failover capabilities.

The architecture consists of:

Production Layer

Responsible for:

• Primary service delivery
• Customer workloads
• Operational processing

Responsible for:

• Data synchronization
• Configuration replication
• Backup creation

Recovery Layer

Responsible for:

• Standby environments
• Recovery automation
• Service restoration

Validation Layer

Responsible for:

• Recovery verification
• Integrity testing
• Operational readiness validation

Governance Layer

Responsible for:

• Recovery planning

• Compliance reporting

Summary

The Disaster Recovery Architecture provides a comprehensive framework for restoring critical services and maintaining business continuity across the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. Through resilient backup strategies, multi-region replication, Infrastructure-as-Code recovery processes, automated failover mechanisms, continuous monitoring, and regular testing, the architecture minimizes operational disruption and supports rapid recovery from a wide range of disaster scenarios. This architecture serves as a foundational component of organizational resilience, customer trust, and enterprise operational excellence.
