Architecture ID: ARCH-005

Title: Data Lake Design

Business Requirements

The Data Lake serves as the centralized data platform for storing, processing, analyzing, and governing structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data generated throughout the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The architecture supports operational analytics, AI evaluation, retrieval quality analysis, monitoring, audit reporting, machine learning workflows, and business intelligence initiatives.

The platform must satisfy the following business requirements:

• Centralized data storage
• Scalability for petabyte-scale growth
• Support for AI and analytics workloads
• Cost-efficient storage management
• Data governance and lineage
• Multi-tenant data isolation
• Secure access controls
• Disaster recovery readiness
• Compliance support
• Integration with evaluation and monitoring systems

The Data Lake serves as the authoritative repository for operational, analytical, and AI-related datasets.

Architecture Overview

The architecture follows a modern cloud-native data lake pattern built on Amazon S3 and supporting AWS services. Data is ingested from operational systems, transformed through processing pipelines, governed through metadata services, and consumed by analytics, reporting, evaluation, and AI platforms.

The architecture consists of:

Storage Architecture

The Data Lake follows a multi-zone storage model.

Raw Zone

Stores original source data.

Characteristics:

• Immutable storage
• Minimal transformation
• Source preservation

Processed Zone

Stores transformed and validated data.

Characteristics:

• Standardized schemas
• Cleansed datasets
• Operational analytics support

Curated Zone

Stores business-ready datasets.

Characteristics:

• Optimized access patterns
• Reporting readiness
• Governance controls

Archive Zone

Stores long-term retention datasets.

Characteristics:

• Lower-cost storage
• Compliance retention support
• Historical analysis capability

Data Ingestion Architecture

Step 1

Data source generates records.

Step 2

Ingestion service validates payloads.

Step 3

Metadata is assigned.

Step 4

Security controls validate compliance requirements.

Step 5

Data is stored in the Raw Zone.

Step 6

Transformation workflows process records.

Step 7

Processed and Curated datasets are generated.

Summary

The Data Lake Design provides a scalable, secure, and governed foundation for storing and managing enterprise data across the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. Through centralized storage, structured processing pipelines, metadata-driven governance, strong security controls, and comprehensive observability, the architecture enables analytics, AI evaluation, operational reporting, and long-term business intelligence while supporting compliance, resilience, and future growth.
