Architecture ID: ARCH-003

Title: Multi Region Strategy

Business Requirements

The Multi Region Strategy defines the architectural approach for ensuring business continuity, high availability, disaster resilience, geographic redundancy, and operational continuity across the AetherAI Cloud platform. As enterprise customers increasingly depend on AI-driven services for mission-critical workloads, the platform must remain resilient against regional infrastructure failures, cloud service disruptions, network outages, and large-scale operational incidents.

The architecture must satisfy the following business requirements:

• Regional failure tolerance
• High service availability
• Disaster recovery readiness
• Regulatory compliance support
• Reduced operational risk
• Global customer support
• Data durability
• Business continuity assurance
• Scalable growth across regions
• Low recovery times

The strategy applies to all core services including the Enterprise RAG Platform, LLM Gateway, Evaluation Engine, Reliability Platform, Knowledge Hub, Agent Workflow Studio, and supporting infrastructure components.

Architecture Overview

The AetherAI Cloud platform adopts a multi-region deployment model using AWS infrastructure. Production workloads are distributed across a Primary Region and one or more Secondary Regions capable of supporting failover operations.

The architecture follows a regional redundancy model that provides:

• Active-Active services where required
• Active-Passive services where operationally appropriate
• Cross-region replication
• Automated backup strategies
• Disaster recovery orchestration

Regional deployments are designed to operate independently while maintaining synchronization of critical data and configurations.

Regional Design

Primary Region

The Primary Region hosts:

• Customer-facing APIs
• Inference Platform
• Retrieval Platform
• Evaluation Platform
• Administrative systems
• Operational services

The Primary Region serves the majority of production traffic during normal operations.

Secondary Region

The Secondary Region hosts:

• Standby infrastructure
• Replicated datasets
• Recovery services
• Backup repositories

Depending on workload requirements, the Secondary Region may process production traffic or remain in warm standby mode.

Optional Additional Regions

Additional regions may support:

• Geographic expansion
• Regulatory requirements
• Customer-specific deployments
• Advanced resilience strategies

Failover Architecture

Failover triggers include:

• Regional outages
• Severe degradation
• Connectivity failures
• Security events

Failover process:

Step 1

Failure detection.

Step 2

Health verification.

Step 3

Traffic redirection.

Step 4

Service validation.

Step 5

Customer communication if required.

Failover decisions may be automated or manually approved depending on incident severity.

Summary

The Multi Region Strategy provides a resilient architectural framework for maintaining availability, business continuity, and disaster recovery readiness across the AetherAI Cloud platform. By leveraging regional redundancy, automated replication, failover capabilities, comprehensive monitoring, and standardized security controls, the organization can withstand regional failures while continuing to deliver reliable AI services to customers. This architecture serves as a foundational component of the platform's long-term reliability and operational resilience strategy.
