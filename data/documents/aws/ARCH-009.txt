Architecture ID: ARCH-009

Title: Evaluation Pipeline

Business Requirements

The Evaluation Pipeline provides the quality assurance and reliability validation framework for all AI-powered services operating within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. The platform continuously evaluates retrieval systems, language models, agent workflows, prompts, guardrails, and production deployments to ensure performance, reliability, safety, and business alignment.

The primary objective is to detect regressions before production deployment, identify hallucinations, measure retrieval quality, validate model behavior, monitor operational performance, and provide automated quality gates within software delivery workflows.

The platform must satisfy the following business requirements:

• Automated AI evaluation
• Regression detection
• Hallucination monitoring
• Retrieval quality validation
• CI/CD integration
• Golden dataset management
• Continuous production monitoring
• Explainable evaluation results
• Cost-efficient operation
• Enterprise auditability

The Evaluation Pipeline serves as a critical control system for the Enterprise RAG Platform, LLM Gateway, Guardrails Gateway, Reliability Platform, Agent Workflow Studio, and associated AI services.

Summary

The Evaluation Pipeline provides the quality assurance backbone for AI systems operating within AetherAI Cloud. Through automated testing, golden datasets, regression detection, hallucination monitoring, deployment gating, and continuous evaluation, the platform ensures that AI services remain reliable, accurate, secure, and aligned with business objectives. The architecture serves as a foundational component of the organization's AI Reliability Platform and enterprise AI governance strategy.
