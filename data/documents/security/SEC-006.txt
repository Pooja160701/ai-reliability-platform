Policy ID: SEC-006

Title: Third Party Access Policy

Objective

The purpose of this Third Party Access Policy is to establish security, governance, and operational requirements for granting, managing, monitoring, reviewing, and revoking access provided to external organizations and individuals interacting with AetherAI Cloud systems. Third-party access introduces additional security and operational risks that must be managed through formal controls, contractual obligations, access restrictions, monitoring mechanisms, and periodic reviews.

AetherAI Cloud relies on selected vendors, technology partners, consultants, contractors, auditors, managed service providers, and integration partners to support business operations. This policy ensures that external access is granted only when necessary, remains appropriately restricted, and is continuously monitored to protect organizational assets and customer information.

Scope

This policy applies to:

• Contractors
• Consultants
• Managed service providers
• Technology vendors
• Cloud service partners
• External auditors
• Integration partners
• Temporary support personnel
• Security assessment providers
• Professional services organizations

The policy covers access to:

• Production systems
• Development environments
• Testing environments
• Cloud infrastructure
• Databases
• APIs
• Documentation repositories
• Monitoring systems
• Security platforms
• Customer information systems

Policy Statements

Third-party access shall only be granted when a legitimate business need exists.

All third-party access must be:

• Approved
• Documented
• Time-bound
• Monitored
• Periodically reviewed

Access permissions must be restricted to the minimum level necessary to perform approved responsibilities.

Third parties shall not receive unrestricted access to organizational systems.

Access Principles

Least Privilege

Third parties must receive only the permissions necessary for approved work activities.

Examples include:

• Read-only monitoring access
• Limited application support access
• Restricted troubleshooting permissions

Administrative privileges must be avoided unless operationally required.

Need-to-Know

Third parties may only access information directly related to their assigned responsibilities.

Examples:

• Infrastructure support personnel access infrastructure systems only.
• Security assessors access approved testing environments only.
• Integration partners access designated APIs only.

Access Segmentation

Third-party access should be isolated whenever possible.

Segmentation methods include:

• Dedicated accounts
• Network restrictions
• Environment separation
• Project-specific permissions

Shared access arrangements are prohibited.

Third Party Categories

Contractors

Individuals performing services under contractual agreements.

Examples:

• Software engineers
• Platform engineers
• Security consultants

Managed Service Providers

Organizations responsible for ongoing operational support.

Examples:

• Monitoring providers
• Infrastructure support vendors

Technology Vendors

Organizations supporting licensed products and services.

Examples:

• Enterprise software vendors
• Cloud technology providers

External Auditors

Organizations conducting assessments, audits, or compliance reviews.

Examples:

• Security assessors
• Regulatory auditors

Access Approval Requirements

All third-party access requests must include:

• Business justification
• Scope of access
• Duration of access
• Assigned sponsor
• Risk assessment

The sponsoring employee is responsible for validating the necessity of requested access.

Required approvals may include:

• Resource owner
• Engineering manager
• Security team
• Compliance personnel

Access Provisioning

Step 1: Request Submission

The sponsoring employee submits an access request containing:

• Third-party organization
• Individual name
• Requested permissions
• Business purpose
• Access duration

Step 2: Risk Assessment

Security personnel evaluate:

• Data exposure risks
• Operational risks
• Compliance considerations
• Vendor security posture

Step 3: Approval

Authorized reviewers approve or reject the request.

Step 4: Account Creation

Dedicated accounts are provisioned.

Requirements include:

• Unique user identity
• Role-based permissions
• Defined expiration date

Step 5: Access Validation

Verify that granted permissions align with approved requirements.

Authentication Requirements

Third-party accounts must utilize:

• Multi-Factor Authentication
• Unique credentials
• Secure authentication mechanisms

Shared passwords are prohibited.

Privileged access requires enhanced security controls.

Monitoring

Third-party activities must be monitored continuously.

Monitoring includes:

• Login activity
• Administrative actions
• Data access
• Configuration changes
• Privilege escalations

High-risk activities must generate security alerts.

Review Requirements

Third-party access must undergo periodic review.

Review frequency:

• Privileged access: Quarterly
• Standard access: Semi-annually
• Temporary access: Prior to expiration

Reviews must verify:

• Continued business need
• Permission appropriateness
• Compliance with agreements

Unnecessary access must be removed immediately.

Data Protection Requirements

Third parties accessing sensitive information must comply with:

• Data encryption requirements
• Access control policies
• Confidentiality obligations
• Security monitoring requirements

Customer information may only be accessed when explicitly authorized.

Third parties must not retain organizational data beyond approved requirements.

Termination and Revocation

Access must be revoked when:

• Contracts expire
• Projects conclude
• Business needs change
• Security concerns arise
• Compliance violations occur

Access removal should occur immediately following termination events.

Incident Management

Security incidents involving third parties require immediate escalation.

Examples include:

• Unauthorized access
• Credential compromise
• Data exposure
• Policy violations

Incident response procedures must be activated without delay.

Third parties must cooperate fully with investigations.

Exceptions

Exceptions require:

• Business justification
• Security review
• Management approval
• Defined expiration period

Compensating controls must be implemented when standard requirements cannot be met.

Compliance Requirements

Compliance verification includes:

• Access reviews
• Vendor assessments
• Security audits
• Monitoring evaluations
• Contractual compliance reviews

Evidence must be retained according to organizational governance requirements.

Enforcement

The Information Security team is responsible for oversight and enforcement.

Violations may result in:

• Immediate access suspension
• Contract review
• Increased monitoring
• Corrective action requirements
• Termination of third-party access privileges

Serious violations may result in contractual or legal action.

Summary

The Third Party Access Policy establishes a comprehensive framework for securely managing external access to AetherAI Cloud systems and information. Through strict approval processes, least privilege controls, monitoring, periodic reviews, and rapid revocation procedures, the organization minimizes risks associated with vendor and contractor access while supporting legitimate business operations. Compliance with this policy is mandatory for all third-party relationships involving access to organizational resources.
