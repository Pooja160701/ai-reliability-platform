Policy ID: SEC-008

Title: Data Retention Policy

Objective

The purpose of this Data Retention Policy is to establish standardized requirements for the retention, archival, review, and disposal of information managed within the AetherAI Cloud environment. Effective data retention practices support regulatory compliance, operational efficiency, business continuity, security governance, audit readiness, and cost optimization while minimizing risks associated with excessive data storage.

AetherAI Cloud processes and stores operational, customer, security, application, infrastructure, and AI-related information across multiple systems and services. This policy defines how long various categories of information must be retained and how data must be securely archived or disposed of when retention requirements are satisfied.

Scope

This policy applies to:

• Customer information
• Application data
• Operational records
• Audit logs
• Security records
• Database records
• Backup repositories
• Monitoring data
• AI evaluation datasets
• Model metadata
• Infrastructure records
• Documentation repositories

The policy applies across production, staging, development, testing, backup, and disaster recovery environments.

Policy Statements

Information must only be retained for as long as necessary to satisfy:

• Business requirements
• Legal obligations
• Regulatory requirements
• Security needs
• Operational requirements

Data exceeding approved retention periods must be archived or securely disposed of according to established procedures.

Retention decisions must be documented and periodically reviewed.

Data Classification

Public Data

Information approved for unrestricted distribution.

Examples:

• Marketing content
• Public documentation

Internal Data

Information intended for internal organizational use.

Examples:

• Internal reports
• Operational documentation

Confidential Data

Information requiring protection from unauthorized disclosure.

Examples:

• Customer configurations
• Architecture documentation
• Operational metrics

Restricted Data

Highly sensitive information requiring enhanced protection.

Examples:

• Credentials
• Authentication records
• Encryption key metadata
• Security investigation records

Retention controls increase according to data sensitivity.

Retention Categories

Customer Data

Examples:

• Account information
• Service configurations
• Operational metadata

Minimum Retention:

7 years after account closure unless otherwise required by law or contract.

Audit Logs

Examples:

• Authentication events
• Administrative activities
• Access records

Minimum Retention:

7 years

Security Logs

Examples:

• Threat detections
• Security investigations
• Incident records

Minimum Retention:

7 years

Infrastructure Logs

Examples:

• Cloud activity records
• Deployment events
• Configuration changes

Minimum Retention:

3 years

Monitoring and Observability Data

Examples:

• Metrics
• Traces
• Operational dashboards

Minimum Retention:

13 months

Backup Data

Examples:

• Database backups
• System snapshots
• Disaster recovery archives

Retention varies according to backup classification and recovery requirements.

AI Evaluation Data

Examples:

• Benchmark datasets
• Evaluation results
• Reliability metrics

Minimum Retention:

5 years

Documentation Records

Examples:

• Policies
• Standards
• Procedures
• Architecture documentation

Minimum Retention:

Until superseded plus 3 years

Legal Hold Requirements

When legal, regulatory, or investigative requirements exist:

• Standard retention schedules may be suspended.
• Data must not be deleted.
• Additional preservation requirements may apply.

Only authorized legal or compliance personnel may remove legal hold restrictions.

Storage Requirements

Retained data must be stored securely.

Requirements include:

• Encryption at rest
• Access controls
• Backup protection
• Availability protections

Retention repositories must comply with applicable security standards.

Archival Requirements

Data no longer required for active operations may be archived.

Archival controls include:

• Integrity verification
• Access restrictions
• Encryption protection
• Auditability

Archived data remains subject to security and compliance requirements.

Disposal Requirements

Data disposal must be performed securely.

Approved methods include:

• Cryptographic erasure
• Secure deletion procedures
• Controlled destruction of storage media

Deletion activities must be documented when required.

Data must not be recoverable after approved disposal activities are completed.

Roles and Responsibilities

Information Security Team

Responsible for:

• Security oversight
• Retention policy enforcement
• Disposal reviews

Compliance Team

Responsible for:

• Regulatory guidance
• Legal hold management
• Audit support

Service Owners

Responsible for:

• Data classification
• Retention implementation
• Periodic reviews

Platform Operations

Responsible for:

• Storage management
• Backup administration
• Disposal execution support

Step-by-Step Procedure

Step 1: Data Identification

Identify data categories and ownership.

Step 2: Classification

Assign the appropriate classification level.

Step 3: Retention Assignment

Apply approved retention schedules.

Step 4: Storage Validation

Verify secure storage controls.

Step 5: Periodic Review

Review retained data regularly.

Step 6: Archive Determination

Identify data eligible for archival.

Step 7: Disposal Approval

Approve secure disposal activities.

Step 8: Secure Disposal

Execute approved deletion procedures.

Step 9: Documentation

Record retention and disposal activities.

Monitoring

Retention compliance monitoring must include:

• Storage growth
• Retention violations
• Deletion failures
• Archive integrity checks

Significant findings must be investigated.

Exceptions

Exceptions require:

• Business justification
• Compliance review
• Security assessment
• Defined expiration period

Compensating controls must be documented.

Compliance Requirements

Compliance verification includes:

• Retention audits
• Storage reviews
• Disposal validation
• Regulatory assessments

Evidence supporting compliance must be retained according to governance requirements.

Enforcement

The Information Security and Compliance teams are responsible for enforcement.

Violations may result in:

• Corrective action plans
• Increased monitoring
• Audit findings
• Remediation requirements

Intentional destruction of retained records may result in disciplinary action and legal consequences.

Summary

The Data Retention Policy establishes requirements for retaining, archiving, reviewing, and disposing of information throughout its lifecycle within AetherAI Cloud. By aligning retention practices with business, legal, regulatory, and security requirements, the organization protects critical information, reduces unnecessary storage risk, and maintains compliance obligations. Adherence to this policy is mandatory for all personnel, systems, applications, and services operating within the AetherAI Cloud environment.
