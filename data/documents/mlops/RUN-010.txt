Runbook ID: RUN-010

Title: Golden Dataset Update

Purpose

This runbook defines the operational procedures for updating, validating, approving, versioning, and maintaining Golden Datasets within the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. Golden Datasets serve as the authoritative benchmark used to evaluate AI systems, Retrieval-Augmented Generation workflows, agent platforms, safety controls, retrieval quality, groundedness, and overall reliability.

The objective is to ensure that benchmark datasets remain accurate, representative, stable, auditable, and aligned with evolving business requirements while preserving evaluation integrity and historical comparability.

Golden Datasets are foundational to AI governance and directly influence deployment approval decisions, regression detection, reliability scoring, safety validation, and production readiness assessments.

This runbook applies to:

• RAG evaluation datasets
• Groundedness benchmarks
• Hallucination test datasets
• Agent workflow benchmarks
• Retrieval evaluation datasets
• Safety evaluation datasets
• Security testing datasets
• Reliability benchmark datasets

Recovery Procedures

Scenario 1: Dataset Quality Failure

Actions:

1. Suspend publication.
2. Correct quality issues.
3. Revalidate dataset.

Scenario 2: Labeling Error

Actions:

1. Isolate affected records.
2. Review ground truth.
3. Reissue corrected version.

Scenario 3: Benchmark Instability

Actions:

1. Compare historical results.
2. Review modifications.
3. Restore prior version if necessary.

Scenario 4: Governance Failure

Actions:

1. Suspend approval process.
2. Complete governance review.
3. Reassess publication readiness.

Rollback Procedure

Step 1

Deactivate updated dataset.

Step 2

Restore previous approved version.

Step 3

Update evaluation configurations.

Step 4

Validate benchmark stability.

Step 5

Document rollback activities.

Summary

This runbook establishes the standardized operational procedure for maintaining Golden Datasets throughout the AetherAI Cloud ecosystem. Through structured governance, rigorous validation, controlled versioning, benchmark management, and continuous monitoring, the organization ensures that evaluation datasets remain accurate, trustworthy, and representative of real-world conditions. Compliance with this runbook is mandatory for all benchmark datasets supporting AI evaluation, reliability engineering, release governance, and production quality assurance.
